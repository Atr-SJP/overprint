Overprint

How to play

  1. Start in your corner.Your first piece must cover your corner square.
  2. Corners only.Each new piece must touch your own ink at a corner, never along a side. Touching rivals is fine.
  3. Most ink wins.One point per square you own. Print all 21 pieces for +10. The game ends when nobody can move.

Ink drops

Cover a drop to collect it. Each one lets you break one rule, once.

    The third and fourth printers start with a spare drop. The first turn moves one seat clockwise every sheet.

    Die-cut holes can't be printed on. Every sheet is cut differently.
